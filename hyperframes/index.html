Key Takeaways
Welcome to this short demonstration of Word Styles. On screen you see our practice document titled Document Review Summary. The title and headings are manually formatted, but Word treats them as plain paragraphs.
Select the title Document Review Summary and apply the Title style from the Styles gallery. This tells Word that this line is the document's title.
Next, select the major sections — Background, Key Themes, and Recommended Next Steps — and apply Heading 1. These define the main sections.
Now select Responsiveness, Clarity, and Consistency. They belong under Key Themes, so apply Heading 2. Leave the paragraphs beneath as Normal.
Open the Navigation Pane from the View tab. Word now shows a structured outline — major sections at the top level, subsections nested underneath.
To change formatting, right-click the Heading 1 style → Modify, change the font or color, click OK. Every Heading 1 updates automatically.
Styles give Word the structural information it needs. Format by role — not appearance — for consistent, client-ready documents.
